Privacy Policy — CSC Virtual Assistant

Last updated: June 2026

This policy describes how the Community Swim Club ("CSC", "we", "us") handles information collected through our automated assistant, available on Facebook Messenger and as a chat widget on communityswimclub.com.

What we collect

When you send a message to our automated assistant — on the CommunitySwimClub Facebook Page or via the chat widget on our website — we receive and store:

  • The text of your message
  • The text of our automated reply
  • A pseudonymous identifier with no link to your name, email, or social profile:
    • Messenger: a one-way cryptographic hash of your Page-Scoped ID — we cannot reverse this to determine your name, email address, or Facebook profile.
    • Website chat: a one-way cryptographic hash of a random identifier generated by your browser for the chat session. It is stored only in your browser's session storage and is cleared when you close the tab.
  • The timestamp and response time of the exchange
  • Whether the bot was able to answer your question or referred you to a club administrator

We do not collect your name, email address, social media profile, or any other personal information beyond what is listed above.

Website chat and rate limiting

The website chat widget is open to anyone visiting communityswimclub.com without signing in. To prevent automated abuse and keep our costs under control, requests to the chat widget are rate-limited by IP address. IP addresses are used transiently for this purpose and are not stored in our conversation records.

How we use this information

We use conversation data solely to:

  • Deliver an automated response to your question
  • Monitor the quality and accuracy of the automated assistant
  • Understand which topics members ask about most so we can improve our knowledge base

We do not use this data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not share it with any third party for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

Conversation data is processed by the following third-party services operating on our behalf:

  • Anthropic — the AI model that generates responses. Messages are sent to Anthropic's API and are subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Langfuse — an analytics platform we use to monitor response quality and detect errors. Data sent to Langfuse includes message text and the pseudonymous identifier described above, and is subject to Langfuse's Privacy Policy.

Both services are used only to operate and improve the automated assistant.

Data retention

Conversation records are retained for 12 months and then deleted. You may request earlier deletion of your records by contacting us at the address below.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any conversation records associated with your account by contacting us at secretary@communityswimclub.com. Because we store only a pseudonymous identifier (not your name or email), we may ask you to provide the approximate date and content of your conversation so we can locate the record.
